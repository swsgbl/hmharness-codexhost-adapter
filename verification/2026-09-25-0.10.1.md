## Migration

- The request-bridge warmup retry.
- Plugin-thread ownership restoration.
- The dedicated HMHarness SVG and no Antigravity fallback.
- The idempotent production-bundle i18n gate patch.
- Chrome extension-host config and Native Messaging wrapper restoration.
